## 1. Bob Skerritt — Car #1049 (1971 Sprint FHC, chassis 7105130266E)

**To:** `bloodknock@hotmail.com`
**Subject:** Your Elan's photos have gone missing from the Registry

Hi Bob,

I'm sorry to say we've lost the photos for your 1971 Sprint FHC
(7105130266E) on the Elan Registry. Your car's entry is fine — the
details, history and chassis record are all intact — but the 2 photos
attached to it are no longer on our server.

This was our fault, not anything you did. A flaw in how our site saved
uploaded images meant the photos were recorded against your car but never
actually stored properly. We've found the cause and it's being fixed so
it can't happen again. Unfortunately the original files can't be
recovered, which is why I'm writing.

**Would you be willing to upload them again?** If you still have the
photos, it only takes a few minutes:

1. Go to the sign-in page and use **"Sign in with Email"** — no password
   needed. Enter this address and we'll send you a link that signs you
   straight in. It's good for 15 minutes; if it says expired, just ask
   for another.
2. Open your car's entry and add the photos as you did before.

If you no longer have the originals, that's absolutely fine — just let me
know and I'll clear the empty photo slots so your entry displays tidily.

And if anything gets in your way, reply to this email and I'll help you
through it, or take the photos by email and upload them for you.

With apologies for the loss, and thanks for being part of the Registry.

All the best,
Jim
